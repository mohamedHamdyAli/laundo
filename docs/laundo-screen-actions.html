Tracking

2 screens — the busiest actions in the app

تتبع الطلب

Feed
GET/api/v1/orders/{id}/trackthe timeline, the driver card, the live dot GET/api/v1/orders/{id}the pricing lines, the QR, handover, ETA GET/api/v1/orders/{id}/reviewestimated vs final counts
Action
POST/api/v1/orders/{id}/confirmaccepts the re-count — this is the step the order is waiting on POST/api/v1/orders/{id}/disputerejects it, and opens a complaint POST/api/v1/orders/{id}/queriesanswers a question staff asked PUT/api/v1/orders/{id}/cancelonly while it is still cancellable POST/api/v1/orders/{id}/reschedulemoves the window POST/api/v1/orders/{id}/ratingafter delivery
Panel
POST/admin/order/review/{id}the count that turns the estimate into a final price POST/admin/order/query/{id}asks the customer something POST/admin/order/task/release/{id}takes a leg off a driver /admin/order/show/{id} · /admin/driver · /admin/complaint
The two pairs to hold onto. Staff post /admin/order/review/{id}; the customer answers with /orders/{id}/confirm or /orders/{id}/dispute. Staff post /admin/order/query/{id}; the customer answers with /orders/{id}/queries. Both halves are live — neither is a stub.

six, not fiveThe timeline has six points and this screen draws five. «تم تأكيد السعر» stays: it is the only step waiting on the customer, and hiding it hides why nothing is moving. Two other mocks in the file already include it.

third call«تم العثور على قطعة إضافية» cannot be drawn from /track. It needs /orders/{id}/review and a comparison of estimated.items_count against final.items_count; the operator's sentence is that response's note.

driver cardName, rating and phone come from /track — the number only while that leg is live, gated exactly as the live dot is. Address, ETA and handover come from /orders/{id}, so the card as drawn needs both. There is no chat route anywhere: the message button has nothing behind it and should come off the design.

تم الدفع بنجاح

Feed
GET/api/v1/orders/{id}/paymentspolled until paid turns true; «رقم المعاملة» is transaction_reference
Action
POST/api/v1/payments/webhook/{provider}not the app's call — the gateway's. It is the only thing that marks an order paid.
Panel
/admin/payment · /admin/order/invoice/{id}

why pollingA payment settles on the webhook and on nothing else — not on the redirect the customer returns through, which they can close. So this screen has to wait on /payments; it is never told directly.
